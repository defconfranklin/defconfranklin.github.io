This repo is for the website of defconfranklin.com.

The National Rural Water Association and DEF CON Franklin launched the Water Watch Center (WWC) in August 2026 at Def Con 34 in Las Vegas to provide round-the-clock cybersecurity support and threat monitoring for small U.S. water utilities.

Key Details Target Audience: Small water utilities serving 10,000 or fewer people, which make up 91% of community water systems in the U.S.Core 
Purpose: Detect cyber threats, share patches, and mitigate attacks from domestic and international adversaries targeting critical water infrastructure.
Partner Providers: Five managed detection and response providers—Defendify, Legato Security, L1 Secure, Rapid7, and Sentinel Technologies—collaborate with the National Rural Water Association as the national hub.
